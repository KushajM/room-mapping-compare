What this comparison does, and doesn't, establish
  • Reference. The blue column is each hotel's own published room list, scraped from its website. A supplier name is placed in a room only by a strict exact-match rule, never by any of the three systems, so none of them is graded against its own output. Names the rule can't place sit in "Not mapped by the strict rule" and are not scored.
  • Frame. 44 hotels where Vervotech and Plexe v2 both have saved outputs and the names also appear in our traffic panel. Only about 38% of those hotels' benchmark names appear in the panel, so each hotel shows a subset of its rooms.
  • Not rankable yet. Hotel-level bootstrap intervals overlap for every pair of systems. The frame is too small; the next step is running Vervotech and Plexe v2 on the 1,203 hotels of the full benchmark.
  • Name-keyed outputs. Vervotech's and Plexe v2's saved outputs are keyed by room name, so two listings with the same name always land together. That makes identical-name pairs free for them; the headline therefore uses pairs with different names.
  • Shared source. The overlay consults the same hotel room lists the reference is built from, through a different, looser matcher. That tilts this frame toward the overlay, not away from it.
  • Prices. Each name's percentage is its average price in our traffic relative to other rooms quoted in the same search at the same hotel, re-centred so 0% is the hotel's typical room. Raw prices mix currencies and stay lengths, so no absolute amounts are shown. A group's spread (highest minus lowest member percentage) is a diagnostic for eyeballing suspicious merges, not a score: putting every name in its own group would give zero spread.
  • Not a same-date comparison. The incumbent column is an older production version (served July–August 2026) than the Plexe v2 column (deployed policy of 20 September 2026).
  • Vervotech was called through a demo configuration with synthetic hotel identifiers; its production integration may behave differently. Plexe v2's outputs come from the saved benchmark run of the deployed policy.
